Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Information We Collect

We collect the details you provide when registering as a BBite courier, such as your name, student ID (NIM), email, phone number, student card photo, and bank or e-wallet account used for payouts.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Verify your identity and courier eligibility on campus.
  • Match you with orders and process delivery earnings and withdrawals.
  • Send order alerts, security updates, and support responses.

3. Location Data

Your location is used only during active delivery shifts to assign nearby orders and let customers follow their delivery. It is not tracked while you are offline.

4. Data Sharing

Customers and sellers only see the information needed to complete an order, such as your name and delivery status. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You may request to view, update, or delete your data at any time by contacting support@bbitecampus.id. Deleting your data may end your courier access.
